Two competing ageing channels of lithium iron phosphate cathodes
External validation on the Severson et al. fast-charging dataset
========================================================================

Single-feature lifetime model (published vs reproduced RMSE, cycles):
  published  : (103, 138, 196)
  reproduced : (104, 138, 196)   [to within one cycle]

Cycle-life distribution:
  Severson  : n=123, median 742, range 300-2237
  simulated : n=325, median 2014, range 322-8000

Square-root interphase relation under fast charging:
  k_sqrt median = -1.54e-03  (negative for every cell -> law fails)
  frac k_sqrt positive = 0.0 over full fade to 80 % EOL

Early-life fingerprint:
  Spearman correlation = -0.75  (capacity-loss vs cycle life)

Small-sample retraining (41-cell split):
  ten-feature elastic-net test1 RMSE > single-feature 138  (overfits)
  deep network MLP ~ elastic-net  (no gain over single feature)
